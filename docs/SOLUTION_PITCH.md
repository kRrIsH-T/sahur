# Sahur

**Use Windows in the language you speak.**

Computer tasks often assume you can type easily, find your way through menus, and understand the words on the screen. Those steps can make an online form or an everyday task harder for someone with limited mobility, low vision, or little experience using a PC.

Sahur is a voice assistant for Windows that understands Indian languages and mixed speech such as Hinglish. Press **Ctrl+Space** and speak naturally. Say “folder banana sikhao” to get one spoken step at a time with the relevant control highlighted, or “form bharne mein madad karo” to have Sahur ask for and enter each answer while you follow along on screen.

The desktop widget shows when Sahur is listening or replying. Sahur uses Gnani for speech recognition and spoken replies, and handles common commands on the PC. The guide shows where to click so people can learn the steps and repeat them on their own.
